Kudi Shelve — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Kudi Shelve is a budgeting app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your financial information stays on your phone, and the developer cannot see it.

We do not collect, transmit or store your personal data. Kudi Shelve has no user accounts, no sign-up and no servers. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device.

Information the app holds on your device

To do its job, the app stores the following in a database on your phone:

  • Accounts you create, their names and balances
  • Budget categories and the amounts you assign to them
  • Transactions: amounts, dates, categories, payees and notes
  • Payments you have started and not yet confirmed
  • The names of bank apps you choose to open from Kudi Shelve
  • App settings, such as your currency and whether the app lock is on

This information is never uploaded. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Receipts and the camera

When you attach or share a payment receipt, the app reads the text in that image using Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. The image is processed on your phone and is not uploaded by Kudi Shelve. Only the details you confirm (amount, date, payee, reference and any bank charge) are saved to your device. Access to your photos or camera is used only for receipts you choose, and only at the moment you choose them.

Your bank apps

Kudi Shelve never connects to your bank, never asks for your bank login, and cannot move money. When you pay, the app copies the amount to your clipboard and opens the bank app you selected. You complete the payment inside your own bank app, under its own security. To let you pick a bank app, Kudi Shelve reads the list of apps on your phone that have a launcher icon. That list is not recorded or sent anywhere; only the bank apps you add are saved.

Backups you create

Backup and CSV export create a file containing your data and hand it to whichever app you choose, such as Google Drive, email or a messaging app. From that point, the file is in your hands and governed by that service's own privacy policy. Kudi Shelve does not upload backups automatically.

Permissions the app uses

  • Biometrics: to unlock the app with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN, if you turn the app lock on.
  • Photos and camera: to read a receipt you choose.
  • App visibility (queries): to show a list of your apps so you can pick the bank apps you pay with.

Analytics, ads and tracking

Kudi Shelve contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Nothing you do in the app is reported to the developer or to third parties. Google Play may collect its own statistics about installs and crashes, as it does for every app on the Play Store; see Google's privacy policy for details. Google ML Kit, which performs the on-device text recognition, may collect limited technical diagnostics; see Google ML Kit's terms.

Children

Kudi Shelve is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Your control over your data

You can edit or delete any entry in the app at any time. To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings. Because the developer never receives your data, there is nothing to request or delete elsewhere.
